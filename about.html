me & design

design bio

As the informational canvas continues to evolve, I am fascinated with developing new ways to connect and engage people. I aspire to design with a purpose—to rouse conversation, inform, and establish meaningful relationships.

With a BFA in Graphic Design from California State University, Long Beach and over 15 years of agency and independent experience (including Deutsch, M&C Saatchi, and Dailey), my work spans brand identity systems, print and digital campaigns, and custom websites for businesses, cultural institutions, and nonprofits.


social media and small animal advocacy

I created Smalls of San Pedro, an Instagram account dedicated to promoting Harbor Shelter's small animals. My responsibilities include photographing the animals, writing engaging adoption profiles, and advocating for improved small animal care by sharing educational content and resources.

I also launched a monthly adoption event and design the promotional materials to boost visibility and engagement.
